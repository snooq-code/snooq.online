Who we are

The People Behind the Brand

El Dorado Shipping is an international freight forwarding and project logistics company specializing in Out-of-Gauge (OOG), oversized, heavy-lift, and project cargo.

We provide reliable service from standard shipments to complex industrial cargo, we deliver safe, efficient, and cost-effective logistics solutions tailored to every customer’s needs.

Supported by a trusted network of carriers and logistics partners, we offer end-to-end services including freight forwarding, customs clearance, breakbulk, Flat rack, Open-top , warehousing, and multimodal transportation.
